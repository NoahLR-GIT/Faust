Privacy Policy

Faust Gmail Integration · last updated 6 October 2026

Summary

This application is a private tool operated by a single individual for that individual's own mailbox. It has one user, and that user is the person who authorized it. No data gathered by this application is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

What information is accessed

With the account holder's explicit consent, the application requests access to:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify, gmail.send) to read, label, and send mail in the account holder's own mailbox.
  • Google Drive to read and file the account holder's own documents.
  • Google Calendar to read and manage the account holder's own events.
  • Google Tasks to read and manage the account holder's own task lists.
  • Basic profile (openid, email) to confirm which account is connected.

How the information is used

  • To summarise and act on the account holder's own messages at their request.
  • To draft and send email that the account holder has reviewed and approved.
  • To keep the account holder's own files, calendar, and tasks in order.

How the information is stored and protected

  • OAuth credentials are stored as files on the account holder's own computer, readable only by that user account.
  • Message content is processed to produce the account holder's own summaries and drafts. There is no project-operated server, so nothing is transmitted to one.
  • Requests to Google use HTTPS. Credentials are never printed to logs or shared.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's access.

Sharing

Information obtained through Google APIs is not transferred to third parties. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. It is not used to train machine learning models.

Faust's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Cached content is retained only as long as it is useful to the account holder and can be deleted by them at any time. Revoking access, or deleting the stored credential files, removes the application's ability to reach the account. To request deletion of any retained copy, contact the address below.

Children

This application is not directed at children and has no users under 18.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by the date at the top of this page.
